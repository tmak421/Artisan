## Log Format

Each entry must capture:
- **Date** — YYYY-MM-DD
- **Hours** — Time spent on qualified research
- **Project / Component** — Name of the product or feature under development
- **Technical Problem** — What specific problem was being solved
- **Technical Uncertainty** — What was unknown or unresolved at the start of the session
- **Experimentation Approach** — How alternatives were evaluated, tested, or modeled
- **Tools & Platforms** — All platforms and APIs used
- **Outcome / Current State** — Result or status at end of session
